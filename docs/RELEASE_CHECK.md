# Public-site quality checklist

1. Open https://mohamedtammam-geo.github.io/govision-earth/ in a private browser window.
2. Confirm satellite imagery displays without watermarks; switch to Streets.
3. Test three dates and all six radar views.
4. Test Single, Side by side, Swipe and GIF timeline. In Swipe drag the divider and move both synchronized views; test the handle with keyboard arrows.
5. Confirm hero GIF rotates through all three dates with the correct area values.
6. Confirm light/dark theme and map-label toggles, opacity and extent reset.
7. Switch F1, Precision, Recall and F2; switch the four individual area series and All four.
8. Download CSV and verify exported data.
9. Test at mobile width and confirm visible legend, credits, and map controls.
10. Read the Scientific Method page for the scope of the Sentinel-2 optical comparison.

If a basemap provider cannot be reached in a browser, switch to the other basemap. Basemap access depends on third-party services; NISAR thematic map files are bundled locally.
